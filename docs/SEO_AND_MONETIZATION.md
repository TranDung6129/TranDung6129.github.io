# SEO, Distribution & Monetization Principles

## Channels and intent
- **Evergreen search:** explanations/tutorials and chapters.
- **Timely search:** factual news and versioned technology changes with dates/sources.
- **Commercial search:** authentic product comparison/technical reviews.
- **YouTube:** narrative films; articles house transcript, reference list, experiments.
- **TikTok/Shorts:** intriguing, honest short clips leading to relevant companion pages.

## Phase 1 SEO foundation
- Capture current URLs before migration; keep them stable where possible.
- Unique titles/descriptions, valid canonicals, sitemap/robots/RSS, structured data appropriate to page.
- OG images and accessible alt text; semantic heading hierarchy.
- Hreflang only for verified translated equivalents.
- Validate live URL targets and analytics/consent constraints; no fabricated freshness timestamps.
- Google Search Console, analytics and UTM strategy prepared, not configured with secrets in repo.
- Prioritize information architecture, internal linking, helpful original content and performance.

## Monetization design (future, not implemented now)
### Affiliate / Discover
Product discovery inspired by short-form video behavior, but supported by real testing, specs, alternatives and transparent caveats. Distinguish editorial recommendations from affiliate placement; mark sponsored/affiliate clearly. No invented prices, ratings or hands-on experience. External provider handles checkout. Follow applicable local commercial/tax/advertising requirements.

### Reader support / membership
- Primary pitch: voluntarily back thoughtful independent publishing.
- One-time and recurring support are possibilities; do not default to coercive paywalls.
- Optional premium essays, early access and supporting files only after content proves value.
- Public static HTML can never securely hold subscriber-only article bodies.
- Real billing, authenticated entitlements, customer privacy and cancellation require a trusted provider/backend and separate project approval.

### Digital goods
Original guides, source code, notebooks and templates only if distribution rights are owned. Use verified checkout/delivery provider or proper secured service; never trust client-only payment confirmation.

### Advertising
- AdSense considered after editorial content, policy readiness and relevant traffic are demonstrated.
- There is no universal guaranteed traffic approval threshold; obtain current policy review before integration.
- Keep ads visually restrained, label them, reserve layout space to minimize CLS, and never interrupt proofs, code or procedural steps.
- Handle consent/privacy and site policy pages before serving personalized ads where required.

## Metrics
Search impressions/clicks and quality, retained readers, completed reads, returning visitors, newsletter opt-ins, video-to-article visits, affiliate link click-through, attributable conversions (when data available), supporter retention, RPM and editorial trust signals.
Do not optimize comments/clicks at the expense of accuracy.
