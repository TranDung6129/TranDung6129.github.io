## Personal — pieces of a life

A space for personal thoughts, experiences and views on life. From everyday observations to longer reflections, each piece is a small part of trying to understand oneself and the world.

### Topics

- **Journal**: thoughts and experiences from day to day
- **Books and films**: reviews and responses to works read or watched
- **Travel**: journeys and discoveries
- **Self-development**: learning, habits, productivity
- **Reflections**: questions about the meaning of life
