**The Epistemic Diary** is an independent publication about how we come to understand the world: mathematics, computer science, physics, economics and philosophy — and the places where they meet. The name says what we care about most: not only *what* we know, but *how* we know it.

## What you will find

- **Journal** — essays and notes: a question, a model that answers it, and the limits of that model.
- **Volumes** — series arranged as the chapters of a book, for following one idea further.
- **Films** — visual explanations for ideas that are best seen as well as read.
- **Labs** — experiments and interactive models to try for yourself.
- **Discover** — books and papers cited in our essays.

## How we work

- We distinguish what is demonstrated from interpretation, hypothesis and editorial opinion.
- Claims carry their sources; we do not publish numbers we cannot support.
- We are explicit about uncertainty and open questions.
- When we are wrong, we correct it publicly and record the update date.
- There are no ads, affiliate links or sponsored pieces on the site. If that ever changes, they will be clearly labelled.

The publication is at an early stage: many current essays are drafts that will be edited, and several English versions still carry the Vietnamese text.

## Who writes

Essays are currently written by **TranDung**. Coming from mathematics and computer science, he is interested in how an abstract model becomes a computing system, how it survives the noise and error of the physical world, how it explains economic behaviour under uncertainty — and in philosophy as the place where the premises get checked.
